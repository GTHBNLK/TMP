## B. Text changes, in the client's order

### 1. Hero headline

1. **Current:** "Complimentary: Year-End & 2027 Investment Strategy Conversation with Bryan Perry"
2. **Change to**, centered across three lines:
   1. "Complimentary:"
   2. "2027 Investment Strategy Conversation"
   3. "with Bryan Perry"
3. **Look:** bold, with the word "with" in italics.

### 2. Heading after "Let's start with a conversation."

1. **Current:** "Complimentary: A Year-End & 2027 Investment Strategy Conversation with Bryan Perry"
2. **Change to**, across two lines:
   1. "Complimentary: A 2027 Investment Strategy"
   2. "Conversation with Bryan Perry"
3. **Note:** the email says "centered", but the sample is left-aligned like the current heading. Confirm the alignment with the client.

### 3. Paragraph after that heading

1. The first two sentences change. The rest of the paragraph stays as it is.
2. **Current (first two sentences):** "Year-end strikes me as an appropriate time to discuss market conditions and investment strategies. That might mean leaving investments where they are through year-end and beyond or not."
3. **Change to:** "Now strikes me as an appropriate time to discuss market conditions and investment strategies. That might mean leaving investments where they are or not."
4. **Full paragraph after the change:**

   > Now strikes me as an appropriate time to discuss market conditions and investment strategies. That might mean leaving investments where they are or not. It might mean shifting the balance between income and growth. Which one depends entirely on your circumstances, and this complimentary conversation with me could help widen your perspective.

### 4. Last paragraph before the sign-off

1. **Current:** "If you'd like to chat with me, you can request a complimentary, no-obligation Year-End & 2027 Investment Strategy Conversation by completing the request form."
2. **Change to:** "If you'd like to chat with me, you can request a complimentary, no-obligation 2027 Investment Strategy Conversation by completing the request form."
3. The second sentence ("Either my associate or I will connect with you…") stays as it is.

### 5. Order form intro (box on the right)

1. **Current:** "YES! I want a complimentary and confidential Year-End & 2027 Investment strategy conversation with Bryan Perry."
2. **Change to:** "YES! I want a complimentary and confidential 2027 Investment strategy conversation with Bryan Perry."
3. **Look:** "YES!" large and dark navy as now. The rest is bold, with the word "with" in italics.
